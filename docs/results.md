# Results so far

The channel is days old, so this is a record of *method*, not a growth story.

| | |
|---|---|
| Launched | 2026-11-18 |
| Shorts published | 5 (4 in the first batch, 1 from the automated flow) |
| Views after 3 days (first batch) | 8, 8, 21, 12 = 49 |
| Average % viewed | 104.6 %, 39.9 %, 37.3 %, 60.2 % |
| Best owner rating of a first cut | 4 / 5 |
| Human approvals required per Short | 1 (publish) |

What the numbers did change:

* All first-batch titles were long narrative sentences. Studies of top Shorts
  show short, specific titles (26-45 chars, one emoji) doing better, so every
  title was rewritten and the before/after views/day is being tracked.
* The first batch was cut on peaks alone. The owner's ratings of those clips
  (1-2/5) led directly to the humor gate.
* The 5th Short was produced end-to-end by the gated flow; its 48-hour numbers
  go into `data/results.csv` on 2026-11-23.
